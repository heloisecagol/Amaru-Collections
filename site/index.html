Why Amaru

“Most museum-shop jewelry looks nothing like what is in the case. We wanted to change that.”

— The founders [PLACEHOLDER quote & names]

  1. I.

    Faithful to the originals

    Every design starts from a specific archaeological piece. We study its form, proportions and surface detail, recreate it as closely as the craft allows, and credit the source.

  2. II.

    Handmade by Colombian artisans

    Each pair is made by hand in Colombian workshops, using filigree, lost-wax casting and pre-Columbian alloying methods.

  3. III.

    Gives back to communities

    Part of every sale supports the economic development of the artisan communities who make the pieces. [PLACEHOLDER: percentage and how it is reinvested]
